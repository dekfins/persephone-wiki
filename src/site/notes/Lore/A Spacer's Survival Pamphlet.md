# SOL, 2526
*A Spacer's Survival Pamphlet*

#### Welcome, passer-by.
Humanity never cracked lightspeed, but we got very good at burning things, killing each other, and flinging ourselves across the void. Earth is a giant Minecraft wheat farm. The planets are factories, fuel depots, and prisons. Weeks ago, a rock from another star showed up. They're calling it Persephone. Everyone’s gone apeshit. You’re here because you need work, money, or a place to disappear.

#### EARTH: 
The Blue Dot. No longer the industrial hub. Strip-mined and resource-depleted, it's been reborn as the system's breadbasket. Vast agricultural zones produce food that's launched skyward in exchange for the technology that keeps the planet alive. The monopoly is run by the **Terran Agricultural Continuum (TAC)** - known system-wide as **Terragrow**. If you're from Earth, you know one of two things: the smell of real soil, or the desperation of leaving it.

#### LUNA: 
Inhabited by humans centuries ago. Now silent. **Base Luna** went dark fifty years ago after an incident that left zero survivors. Official reports cite containment failure. Unofficial reports cite everything from cult uprisings to alien contact. Nobody's in a hurry to find out which is true. Rumors say that “moon hobbits” still roam around, living in the caverns.

#### MARS: 
Surface: The DMV of the solar system. Certifications, crew licenses, insurance bonds. If it flies, Mars stamped it.
Mars orbit: The dealership. Want to get a ship the legal way? You go here. You'll probably pay more than what the ship is worth. Your wallet will cry.
Phobos: The equivalent of Temu. Has a shipyard and a scrapyard. Builds fast, cheap, high-turnover hulls in bulk. Consumerism at its peak. 
Deimos: The precision forge. Torchships, interceptors, and vessels engineered to outlive their crews. Slow, expensive, irreplaceable.

#### THE ASTEROID BELT:
The equivalent of a back alley in a city. Legal? No. Illegal? Technically not. It's a jurisdictional gray zone where Mars law, Earth law, and Jovian law all end and SCUM law begins. Bridges the inner and outer planets. Has widespread criminal activity, though no one admits it. Houses Ceres, the largest trading hub in the solar system. Want something for cheap? Something that doesn't exist on paper? You go to Ceres.

#### THE JOVIAN SYSTEM:
Low Jupiter Orbit: **Antimatter Station Alfa.** Harvests antiprotons from the magnetosphere. One microgram can power a torchship for a month. One containment breach can erase a habitat. The workers call it “the boombox.” The insurers call it a giant liability. The math keeps it running.

Io: **Hephaestus Station**, known locally as the “Big Heff.” Premier geothermal research hub built inside a caldera. Staffed by engineers with a deathwish, pardoned prisoners, and academics who chase eruption data like a religion. In the Big Heff, when the volcanoes blow, it’s just another day. The surface is sulfur and ash. The station runs on heat, hubris, and hazard pay.

Europa: Ice on top. Ocean underneath. **Base Infernus** sits three kilometers below the shell, running on geothermal taps and sheer will. Officially: extremophile research. Unofficially: find alien life. The ice divers don’t talk about the pressure, the cold, or things that go wrong down there. They just cash their checks.

Ganymede: The Las Vegas of the solar system. The Jovian system’s entertainment and broadcast capital. The moon's surface is barren, but orbiting around it is **Cleopatra Station**. A rotating O’Neill cylinder with artificial gravity, questionable activities, and the Hive - a mesh network of relay balloons broadcasting corporate feeds, news, and Milk Skimming telemetry. The sport is simple: dive into Jupiter’s atmosphere, survive the cream, the brown, or the Big Black, and come back up. Mortality is a coin flip. Respect is earned in dive bars. When a Milkman walks in, the room goes quiet. Fresh produce follows.

Callisto: Quiet. Cold. Sparsely populated with automated ice harvesters and derelict survey rigs. Rumor says the current generation Rocket Man operates from Callisto. Or maybe he's dead. Or maybe he never existed. Place your bets at Cleopatra Station.

#### THE SATURNIAN SYSTEM:
Low Saturn Orbit: **Antimatter Station Bravo**. Same mission as Alfa. Different culture. After the “Bravo Incident” vaporized half the staff and the entire executive board, the station became a bureaucratic hellscape. Every procedure requires seven signatures. Every transfer triggers an audit. You'd drown yourself in paperwork before you get out there.

Titan: The solar system’s gas station and chemical refinery. Methane lakes, ethane crackers, and acetylene fermentation vats producing synthetic wine that tastes like shit and costs half a kidney. If you need fuel, you come to Titan. If you need a hangover and burn your credits, you come to Titan.

Iapetus: No one calls it by its real name. They call it "The Prison." On the equatorial ridge sits **ADX Tartarus**. If you get sent to Tartarus, you never come back. The warden, known only as The Warden, runs this place. No one knows who The Warden is.

#### Things to know:
**1. PERSEPHONE**
> A big rock. Interstellar origins. Captured by the Sun recently. Detected one month ago, currently heading towards its periapsis somewhere around Mars and the Belt. Probably wouldn't come back. In a long time. Everyone's sending probes, ships, and lawyers. Some say it's one of the discoveries of all time. Others say it's a threat. You're still not important enough to get near it. Yet.

**2. FOOD** 
> Kibble is the universal ration. The poor eat the cheap stuff. Tastes like cardboard. The middle class gets KFC-flavored nostalgia dust. The rich get nutritionally dense "exotic" flavors like beef wellington. Fresh produce is reserved for Milkmen, CEOs, and people who own moons. Might as well be a myth you might taste once before you die. 

**3. FUEL** 
> An engine is nothing without its drink. Argon is cheap and everywhere - scoop it from an atmosphere if you have the balls. Deuterium-Helium-3 costs a fortune and requires corporate contracts. Fission pellets are tracked, regulated, and logged. Antimatter… you don’t buy antimatter. You steal it, and then you spend the rest of your life running from the feds, or die trying.

**4. TRAVEL** 
> Torchships are the norm. They accelerate at a tenth of a gee or more and cross the system in days. Hohmann transfers are a historical footnote. If your ship can only manage a few hundred kilometres per second of delta-V and a TWR you’d measure with a calendar, you are slow.

**5. FACTIONS**
> The big cheeses of the system.
> - **Terragrow (TAC):** Earth's agricultural monopoly. Control the food, control the system. Every crate of Kibble has their name on it.
> - **Department of Astrogation and Vessel Endorsement (DAVE):** If it flies, they certified it. Most likely corrupt. Everyone hates on them, but they're indispensable.
> - **Strategic Commerce & Underground Mercantile (SCUM):** Ceres, the asteroid miners, and everything in between. Officially a trade union. Unofficially a criminal syndicate. Deniably both.
> - **Jovian Entertainment and Relay Kombinate (JERK):** Runs Cleopatra Station, the Hive, and Milk Skimming. They own your attention span.
> - **Particle Output Syndicate (POS):** They're the system's only licensed antimatter harvesters. Also they're a piece of shit. They know it.

**6. LASTLY, YOU**
> You're not special. You're not chosen. You're not the hero of this story. You're a spacer with a ship, a debt, and a death wish. The system will chew you up and shit you out. Your job is to make sure you're the one doing the shitting. Good luck. You'll need it.

The galaxy doesn't wait. Try not to die.

*P/S: Lightspeed is a bitch. Comms aren't instant. Call your own shots.*
