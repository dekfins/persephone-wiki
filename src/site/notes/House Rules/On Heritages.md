Every character picks one at creation. No in between. You were born either under a dome or on dirt.

**SPACER**

- **BUFF: Rad-Hardened:** You treat your Rads as 2 stacks lower for penalty purposes. Physical saves caused by Acute radiation get a flat +2 bonus. This makes you resilient to the low-level radiation of reactor maintenance, long hauls, and thin-hulled ships. You are not immune; you’re just harder to poison.

- **DEBUFF: Brittle Bones:** Spacers who grew up in microgravity or low-gravity environments have weaker cardiovascular systems and calcium-depleted skeletons. When you are in a high-gravity environment (above 0.8g, like Earth or during high-g burns above 2g), you suffer a -1 penalty to all Physical checks and Physical Effect saving throws. This doesn’t go away with training—it’s a lifetime of adaptation working against you. You can mitigate it with a g-suit or counterpressure gear, but it’s always there.


**EARTHLING**

- **BUFF: Terra Firma:** You are adapted to a full 1g gravity well and your body is a dense, pressurized tank. You gain a +2 bonus on all Physical Effect saving throws made to resist the effects of high-gravity environments and high-g maneuvers. This includes crushing acceleration burns, punishing aerocapture strains, and resisting G-LOC. Additionally, you do not need to purchase the 10,000 CR “Intensive G Training” certification from DAVE. Your body is already certified.

- **DEBUFF: Softblood:** You are Softblooded. You gain +1 additional Rad whenever you suffer a radiation gain event. Space wants to eat you, and your cells don’t have the repair mechanisms that a belter or a void-born does. You feel every click of the Geiger counter.
