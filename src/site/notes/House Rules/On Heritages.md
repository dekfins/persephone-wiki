Every character picks one at creation. No in between. You were born either under a dome or on dirt.

**SPACER**
- **Rad-Hardened:** For determining Chronic Rad penalties, treat your current Rad stack count as 2 lower (minimum 0). You do not lose actual stacks; only the penalty threshold shifts. You also gain +2 to Physical saves triggered by Acute radiation events.

- **Brittle Bones:** Spacers who grew up in microgravity or low-gravity environments have weaker cardiovascular systems and calcium-depleted skeletons. When exposed to gravity >0.8g or sustained acceleration >2g, you suffer -1 to all Physical checks and Physical saves. Wearing a certified G-suit or counterpressure harness negates this penalty, but requires 1 minute to don properly. If caught off-guard, make a Tech/Int check (DC 10) to seal it in time; failure means the penalty applies until you can secure it.


**EARTHLING**
- **Terra Firma:** You are adapted to a full 1g gravity well and your body is a dense, pressurized tank. You gain +2 to Physical saves against high-G stress, G-LOC, aerocapture forces, and crushing acceleration. You automatically count as holding the DAVE "Intensive G-Training" certification (saves 10,000 CR). This bonus applies only to gravity/acceleration stress, not other environmental hazards.

- **Softblood:** Whenever you gain Rad stacks from any source (reactor leak, solar flare, unshielded transit, Persephone handling, etc.), add +1 to the total Rads gained. This applies to Chronic accumulation and Acute events that specify Rad gain. It does not affect Acute CON loss saves.
