In Persephone, radiation poisoning is divided into Acute and Chronic.

**Acute:**
Radiation is a slow killer in small doses, and a quick end in larger ones. Targets exposed to dangerous amounts of radiation must make a Physical saving throw at intervals depending on the strength of the glow. Mildly dangerous zones might force a save once per day or once per hour, up to lethal rad zones that force multiple saves per round. Vacc suits and other forms of protection can effectively shield a wearer from weaker sources of radiation. 

Each failed saving throw lowers the target’s Constitution score by one point. If the new score decreases a hero’s Constitution modifier, they lose any excess maximum hit points their former modifier granted. Characters reduced below half their original Constitution score will die in 1d6 weeks from radiation poisoning; those reduced below a Constitution score of 3 will die instantly. Radiation poisoning that does not immediately kill a victim can induce vomiting, head- aches, soft tissue bleeding and dizziness, but a resolute victim can continue functioning more-or-less normally until death. A target killed by radiation poisoning cannot be revived by Psychic Succor or other stabilization techniques. 

Someone who has suffered a fatal dose of radiation poisoning can only be saved by treatment at a TL4 hospital or ship’s sick bay, recovering the lost Constitution at a rate of one point per week. Non-fatal doses can be treated by a TL4 medkit and an Int/Heal check at difficulty 10. Each success restores one lost point of Constitution and a check can be made once per day.

**Chronic:**
For low-level, constant exposure radiation, players gain Rads, which stack.

| Rads (stacks) | Penalty                                                                                 |
| ------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 0-3           | No effect. 1-3: roleplay flavor (Geiger clicks, metallic taste)                         |
| 4-5           | -1 to all Phys checks and saves                                                         |
| 6-7           | -1 to all checks and saving throws                                                      |
| 8-9           | -2 to all checks and saves. Vomiting, dizziness ensues                                  |
| 10            | -2 to all checks and saves. Additionally, make 1 Phys save/day, failure lowers Con by 1 |

**Accumulation Methods:**
- **Reactor maintenance:** Each time a character fails a Fix-based skill check during reactor maintenance, they suffer a minor radiation leak. Make a Physical Effect save. Failure: +1 Rad. Critical failure: +2 Rads.

- **Spending time in high-rad zones:** Skimming a gas giant's radiation belts, passing through an unshielded fuel blending station, handling unprocessed Persephone fragments. Measured in hours or days: +1 Rad per hour, or per day if mild.

- **Acute exposure events:** A flare, a fuel cell breach, a nearby antimatter detonation. These trigger the vanilla rule: one big save, Con loss on failure. But they might _also_ add Rads. A bad solar flare might deal +3 Rads _and_ force a separate Con-loss save. Vega would warn it as "threat multiplier."


**Healing:**
- **Rest in a shielded environment:** Most crew quarters have lead-lined berths. Spending a full day in there doing nothing removes 1 Rad.

- **Anti-rad medication:** A dose removes 2 Rads immediately. If the recipient has already taken a dose within the last week, they risk overdose: make a Physical save or be incapacitated for one day. Cost: ~500 CR per dose. A trained physician (or anyone with Heal skill) can administer the dose safely with a successful Int/Heal check, typically DC 8. On a success, the overdose symptoms are completely negated. On a failure, the medication still takes effect but the overdose save must be made as normal. Each successive dose within the same week increases the DC by +2. [[House Rules/On Incapacitation\|On Incapacitation]]

- **Fresh produce:** Fresh food contains antioxidants and cellular repair compounds that Kibble lacks. A single real apple, shared among the crew, might remove 1 Rad from each person and give a +1 to Phys saves for 24 hours.

- **Dialysis/Plasmapheresis:** Hephaestus Station on Io has a geothermal-sourced radiation spa. A week of treatment there purges all Rads and lost Con. Cost: high, or a favor from a Big Heff engineer.

**Modifiers:**
Rad-Hardened: Rads stack to 12 instead of 10. Penalties take place on 6 stacks instead of 4. Physical saves caused by Acute radiation get a flat +2 bonus.

Softblood:  When you suffer an Acute radiation event (fail the Physical save), you gain +1 Rad in addition to the Con loss.
