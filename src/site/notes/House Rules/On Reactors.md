#### Reactors
Every ship has a reactor. It provides the ship's **Power** and carries its own health bar: **Reactor Integrity (RI)**. RI isn't "safety." It's a count of how many catastrophic failures this particular design can suffer before it goes boom. A dumb, ancient reactor might survive six separate screw-ups while bleeding radiation the whole time. A modern, efficient design might fail after only three, but it won't poison you while it's healthy. 

RI decreases through combat damage, critical failures, or extreme events. It can be restored in flight via **Reactor Maintenance** (risky) or fully repaired at a shipyard (expensive). RI can never exceed the reactor's maximum.

---
#### Reactors as Fittings
Every ship has a reactor fitting. The reactor provides **Power** (as per hull class) and has its own health bar, **Reactor Integrity (RI)**. When you buy or salvage a ship, the reactor type determines how much RI you have and what happens when things go wrong.

- **Hashmark (#):** Base cost is for a Fighter. Frigate ×2, Cruiser ×3, Capital ×4.
- **Asterisk (\*):** Base cost is for a Fighter. Frigate ×10, Cruiser ×25, Capital ×100.
